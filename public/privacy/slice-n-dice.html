Privacy Policy · Last updated 22 November 2026

com.novabyte.slicendice

Slice n Dice is published by NovaByte Studios PK ("we"). This policy explains exactly what the game collects and why. It covers this game only; our other games have their own policies.

What we collect

  • A player identifier. If you sign in with Google Play Games we receive a Play Games player id and your Play Games display name. We also create an identifier for your game session so your progress can be matched to you.
  • Your display name, during online matches. If you play against another person, your Play Games display name is shown to them so they know who they are playing against. It is not stored after the match.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card details.
  • Game progress. Levels, stars, coins, gems and settings are stored on your own device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location. The game contains no chat and no way to type free text, so there is no message history to collect.

Analytics

With your permission, we collect anonymous gameplay events together with your device model, operating system and app version, so we can understand which parts of the game people enjoy. You are asked before any of this is collected, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings. Declining does not limit the game in any way.

Advertising

The game may offer optional rewarded videos, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game item. These are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. See Google's policy for how that data is handled. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. There are no banner or interstitial ads.

Children

Slice n Dice is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Slice n Dice, published by NovaByte Studios PK.

To delete your data:

  1. Email wasay6788@gmail.com from the Google account you play with, with the subject "Delete my Slice n Dice data".
  2. We confirm the request and action it within 30 days.

What is deleted: the player identifier we hold for you, and any match or leaderboard records associated with it.
What is kept: records of purchases, where tax or accounting law requires us to retain them. These are held by Google Play, not by us.
On your device: uninstalling the game removes your saved progress, coins, gems and settings immediately. Nothing is retained after an uninstall.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
